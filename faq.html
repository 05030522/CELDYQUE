Volufiline 100%

How Volufiline works and how to use CELDYQUÉ's pure concentrate.

Volufiline What is Volufiline and what does CELDYQUÉ Volufiline 100% concentrate do? +

Volufiline is a patented active ingredient (INCI: Sarsasapogenin) clinically shown to increase subcutaneous adipose tissue volume by up to 8.4% in 4 weeks. CELDYQUÉ Volufiline 100% concentrate delivers this ingredient at full, undiluted potency.

It targets areas that lose volume with age — cheeks, nasolabial folds, under eyes, and lips — acting as a non-invasive volumizing serum. It works by stimulating lipogenesis (fat cell growth) in targeted areas, essentially restoring facial plumpness without injectables.

CELDYQUÉ Volufiline 100% is one of the few consumer products to deliver Volufiline at undiluted concentration — most products contain only 0.5–2%.
Volufiline How do I use CELDYQUÉ Volufiline 100% concentrate? +

CELDYQUÉ Volufiline 100% is a concentrate, not a standalone serum. Here's how to use it:

  • Dispense 1–3 drops into your palm
  • Mix with your moisturizer, cream, or serum before applying
  • Apply to target areas: cheeks, nasolabial folds, under eyes, lips, or brow bone
  • Start once daily (evening); increase to morning and evening once your skin is comfortable
  • For visible results, use consistently for at least 4–8 weeks

Volufiline is compatible with most other actives — niacinamide, peptides, hyaluronic acid — and does not cause photosensitivity, so it can be used day or night.

Volufiline How long until I see results from CELDYQUÉ Volufiline 100%? +

Clinical studies on Volufiline show measurable volumizing effects within 4 weeks of consistent daily use. In the original clinical trial, participants saw an average 8.4% increase in subcutaneous volume after 28 days with twice-daily application.

Most users report visible improvement in plumpness and fullness within 4–6 weeks. Because Volufiline works by stimulating adipogenesis rather than surface-level hydration, results build gradually and continue improving with extended use.

Retinal 0.5

CELDYQUÉ's advanced retinaldehyde serum — how it differs from retinol and how to start.

Retinal What is the difference between Retinal and Retinol? Is CELDYQUÉ Retinal 0.5 better? +

Retinal (retinaldehyde) and retinol are both forms of Vitamin A, but they work at different speeds. The conversion chain in skin is: Retinol → Retinal → Retinoic acid (the active form).

Because retinal is one step closer to retinoic acid, it delivers results approximately 11× faster than retinol at the same concentration. This means CELDYQUÉ Retinal 0.5 can produce visible results — improved skin texture, reduced fine lines, more even skin tone — faster than a comparable retinol product.

Importantly, retinal's greater efficiency means you need less of it to get results — which typically means less irritation than a high-concentration retinol. CELDYQUÉ Retinal 0.5 is designed to be both effective and tolerable.
Retinal Is CELDYQUÉ Retinal 0.5 safe for beginners or people in their 20s? +

CELDYQUÉ Retinal 0.5 can be used by beginners, but as with all retinoids, a gradual introduction is recommended:

  • Start 2–3 nights per week, apply a pea-sized amount to dry skin
  • Avoid using the same night as AHAs, BHAs, or other exfoliants
  • Always use SPF in the morning — retinoids increase photosensitivity
  • After 3–4 weeks without irritation, increase to nightly use

For people in their early 20s: retinoids used preventively (2–3x per week) are appropriate and effective. You do not need to wait until visible aging appears to start.

Retinal Can I use CELDYQUÉ Retinal 0.5 with other actives like niacinamide or AHAs? +

Retinal 0.5 + Niacinamide: Compatible. Niacinamide can actually help reduce the irritation potential of retinoids. Apply niacinamide first, let absorb, then apply Retinal 0.5.

Retinal 0.5 + AHAs/BHAs: Avoid using on the same night. Combine on alternating nights — for example, AHA Monday/Wednesday, Retinal Tuesday/Thursday.

Retinal 0.5 + Vitamin C: Use separately — Vitamin C in the morning, Retinal 0.5 at night.

Niacinamide 20%

High-efficacy Vitamin B3 for pores, pigmentation, and barrier strength.

Niacinamide How do I use CELDYQUÉ Niacinamide 20%? Is 20% concentration too strong? +

CELDYQUÉ Niacinamide 20% delivers Vitamin B3 at a high-efficacy concentration clinically shown to visibly minimize pores, reduce hyperpigmentation, regulate sebum production, and strengthen the skin barrier.

For most skin types, 20% niacinamide is well-tolerated. To introduce safely:

  • Begin with every other day application
  • If mild flushing or tingling occurs, this typically subsides within 1–2 weeks
  • Apply after cleansing, before heavier serums and moisturizer
  • After 2 weeks, increase to daily use morning and/or evening
Niacinamide Can I use CELDYQUÉ Niacinamide with Vitamin C in the same routine? +

Yes. CELDYQUÉ Niacinamide 20% is compatible with Vitamin C. Older concerns about these two ingredients forming niacin (which causes flushing) are largely outdated — at modern usage concentrations and with stable Vitamin C formulations, this reaction is negligible.

Recommended approach:

  • Morning: Vitamin C serum → moisturizer → SPF
  • Evening: CELDYQUÉ Niacinamide 20% → moisturizer

If you have sensitive skin, a split routine (Vitamin C AM, Niacinamide PM) is the most cautious approach.

Azelaic Acid 12%

Multi-functional active for pigmentation, rosacea, and acne-prone skin.

Azelaic Acid What does CELDYQUÉ Azelaic Acid 12% do and how do I use it? +

CELDYQUÉ Azelaic Acid 12% is a multi-functional active that treats hyperpigmentation, rosacea, acne, and post-inflammatory redness simultaneously. At 12%, azelaic acid operates at the prescription-strength threshold used in clinical dermatology.

Key benefits:

  • Hyperpigmentation: Inhibits tyrosinase, reducing melanin overproduction
  • Acne: Antibacterial action against Cutibacterium acnes
  • Rosacea & redness: Anti-inflammatory pathway reduces vascular reactivity

Apply a thin layer to cleansed skin once or twice daily. Unlike most actives, azelaic acid does not cause significant photosensitivity — it can be used in the morning without additional SPF concerns (though daily SPF is always recommended). It is safe for sensitive skin and compatible with niacinamide, peptides, and retinoids.

Bakuchiol

Plant-derived retinol alternative — gentle, effective, and pregnancy-compatible.

Bakuchiol Is CELDYQUÉ Bakuchiol safe during pregnancy and breastfeeding? +

CELDYQUÉ Bakuchiol is a plant-derived retinol alternative from Psoralea corylifolia seed extract. Unlike retinoids, bakuchiol has not been linked to teratogenicity and is widely regarded as a safer option during pregnancy and breastfeeding for those who want anti-aging and skin-renewing benefits.

CELDYQUÉ Bakuchiol provides retinol-like effects — reduced fine lines, improved skin texture, and brightening — without the irritation or pregnancy concerns of retinol.

As with any skincare during pregnancy, we recommend consulting your healthcare provider with specific medical questions, and patch testing before full use.
Bakuchiol Is CELDYQUÉ Bakuchiol as effective as retinol? +

Bakuchiol works via different receptor pathways than retinol, but clinical studies show it delivers comparable improvements in fine lines, skin elasticity, and pigmentation with significantly less irritation. A 2018 study in the British Journal of Dermatology found bakuchiol comparable to 0.5% retinol on key anti-aging metrics after 12 weeks.

CELDYQUÉ Bakuchiol is ideal for those who want retinol-like results with none of the purging, peeling, or downtime — including sensitive skin types and those who cannot use retinoids.

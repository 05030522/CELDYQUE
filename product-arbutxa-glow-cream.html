The Triple Action

This formula attacks hyperpigmentation from three angles. Arbutin (7%) inhibits melanin production, Tranexamic Acid (5%) targets stubborn dark spots, and Niacinamide (2%) prevents pigment transfer.

Together, they work synergistically to fade freckles, blemishes, and dullness for a significantly brighter complexion.

Calm & Hydrate

High potency doesn't mean harsh. We balanced active brighteners with a soothing blend of Centella Asiatica (Cica), Heartleaf, and Green Tea to calm redness.

A multi-depth Hyaluronic Acid Complex and Ceramide NP ensure your moisture barrier stays healthy and resilient against environmental stressors.

How to Use

1

How to Use

1

Prep

After cleansing and toning, take a pea-sized amount of your regular moisturizing cream or lotion onto your palm.

2

Mix

Drop 1 to 2 drops of Volufiline 100% Concentrate into the cream and mix well with your finger.

3

Apply

Gently apply to areas lacking elasticity (cheeks, under eyes, neck, etc.). Pat gently for absorption.

* For body care (breasts, hips), mix 3-4 drops with a generous amount of body lotion.
